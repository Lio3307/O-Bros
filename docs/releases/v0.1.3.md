## O-BROS 0.1.3

### New
- **Keyboard shortcuts for terminals.** Split right (`Ctrl+Shift+D`), split down (`Ctrl+Shift+E`), close the pane (`Ctrl+Shift+W`) and open a new terminal (`Ctrl+Shift+T`). Change any of them in Settings > Shortcuts.
- **Resizable panes remember their size.** Drag the line between two panes, and the sizes come back after a restart.

### Fixed
- **Splitting no longer restarts your terminal.** Splitting a terminal, or closing one pane of several, used to start the other terminals again and lose what you had typed and the folder you had changed to. They now keep running.
- **Terminal text stays inside its pane.** It could run a few columns past the pane's right edge.
- **No more repeated actions from clicking fast.** A button is disabled while its action runs, and ignores a second click right after the first. Holding down a shortcut no longer opens a stream of terminals.
- **Updates start once.** Download and install cannot be started twice, and the update card shows Downloading straight away.
- **Lighter background work.** The Resource Manager reads the process list less often at the same time, and at a lower priority, so it competes less with your typing.

### Good to know
- **Restart and install** reopens O-BROS by itself. This works when you update from 0.1.2 or later, because the older versions close and install without reopening.
- Anything still running in a terminal stops during an update, and your tabs come back as new terminals in the same folders. Claude and Codex keep their conversations, so you can resume them from History.
- The app is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you and macOS will ask you to confirm opening it. macOS cannot update itself: download the new `.dmg`.
